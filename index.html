Common Questions

Is the AI a replacement for a mechanic?

Absolutely not. CarExplain is designed to give you information and confidence. It tells you what *might* be wrong and what to ask your garage. It never replaces a physical inspection by a qualified mechanic.

Can I cancel my Premium subscription?

Yes, you can cancel anytime with one click from your account settings. You'll retain Premium access until the end of your billing cycle.

Where does the MOT data come from?

We use the official DVLA MOT History API. When you enter your registration plate, we pull the official pass/fail records and mileage directly from the government database.
