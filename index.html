Our Dynamic Safety & Trust Commitments

Guidance, Not Certainty: CarExplain provides AI-assisted guidance and crowd-sourced patterns. It does not provide definitive mechanical or physical diagnoses.

Safety Escalation: Critical dashboard alerts or severe control performance failure signs are automatically escalated to guide you to immediate physical inspections.

No Dangerous Work: We never instruct drivers to perform dangerous, uncertified mechanical adjustments yourself. Safety comes first.

Clear Terminology: Built explicitly using trusted UK automotive terms, MOT advisories infrastructure, and local pound sterling (£) cost metrics.
