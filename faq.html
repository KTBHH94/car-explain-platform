General Platform Questions

What exactly is CarExplain? +

CarExplain is a UK-first digital platform designed to help ordinary drivers understand their vehicles before spending money. We combine an AI-assisted diagnostic tool, a digital garage for keeping repair records, a garage quote explainer, and a community forum.

Are you a garage or a mechanic? +

No. We do not perform physical repairs, sell car parts, or provide legally binding mechanical advice. We are an educational and informational software platform. You must always consult a qualified mechanic for physical work.

AI Diagnostics & Safety

How does the AI diagnostic tool work? +

We use a structured question engine to understand your car's symptoms (e.g., when a noise happens, what it sounds like). We then send this anonymised data to a Large Language Model (Google Gemini) to generate a highly detailed, plain-English breakdown of potential causes, safety warnings, and questions to ask a mechanic.

Can the AI guarantee what is wrong with my car? +

No. AI cannot physically inspect your vehicle. It provides probabilities based on the symptoms you describe. Several different faults can produce the exact same symptom. Always have a physical inspection done by a qualified mechanic before authorising expensive repairs.

What if the AI tells me my car is unsafe to drive? +

We have programmed a strict "Safety Gate." If your symptoms match severe, life-threatening faults (such as brake loss, steering failure, or severe overheating), the system will advise you to stop driving immediately. If you ever feel unsafe driving your vehicle, you must stop, regardless of what the AI report says.

Can I add extra details that aren't in the questions? +

Yes! At the very end of every diagnostic session, there is a text box labeled "Anything else we should know?" You can type any extra context (e.g., "It only happens when it rains") which helps the AI make a more accurate assessment.

Quote Checker

Will you tell me if my garage is ripping me off? +

We will never accuse a garage of malicious intent. Instead, the Quote Checker uses neutral, evidence-based language like "within typical range", "higher than typical range", or "significantly higher than typical". We explain why prices vary (e.g., OEM parts vs. aftermarket, main dealer vs. independent, regional labour rates).

Are the prices in the Quote Checker guaranteed? +

No. The prices are indicative and based on UK averages. Actual prices vary significantly based on your specific vehicle model, engine size, your location in the UK, and the quality of parts used. Always obtain a written, itemized quote from your garage before authorising work.

My Garage & Data

Is my data synced across devices? +

Yes. Unlike a simple browser cache, CarExplain uses a secure cloud database (Supabase). When you log in with your email and password on your phone, tablet, or laptop, your digital garage will load with all your saved vehicles and reports.

Where is my data stored? +

Your data is stored securely in our encrypted cloud database (hosted by Supabase). All traffic is routed through secure Cloudflare Workers. We enforce strict Row-Level Security (RLS), meaning User A cannot access User B's private vehicle data.

Will my data be used to train the AI? +

No. We explicitly configure our API integrations with Google so that your symptom data is not retained or used to train public AI models. Your personal details, registration plate, and vehicle history remain private.

How do I delete my account? +

Under UK GDPR, you have the "Right to be Forgotten". You can delete individual vehicles and diagnostic reports directly within your Garage. If you wish to delete your entire account permanently, please email us at hello@carexplain.co.uk from the email address associated with your account.

Community Forum

Is the community forum professional advice? +

No. The community is a place for owners to share their experiences. A post from another user is not a verified mechanical diagnosis. Always treat community advice as anecdotal evidence, not professional fact.

What happens if someone posts spam or abuse? +

We have a strict Acceptable Use Policy. Users can report posts, and our admin team will remove content that violates our community rules. Spam accounts are permanently banned.

Accounts, Billing & Premium

Is CarExplain free to use? +

Yes. You can create a free account to add a vehicle to your garage and run one free AI diagnostic report per month. The community forum and knowledge base are completely free.

What does Premium include? +

CarExplain Premium (£4.99/month) gives you:

  • Unlimited AI diagnostic reports.
  • Unlimited vehicles in your digital garage.
  • Access to the Quote Checker tool.
  • Priority access to new features (like automatic MOT lookups).
How do I cancel my Premium subscription? +

You can cancel at any time via your account settings or by emailing support. Under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, you have a 14-day right to cancel for a full refund, provided you have not already used the Premium service.

We are currently applying for integration with the DVSA (Driver & Vehicle Standards Agency) official API. Once live, Premium users will be able to enter their registration plate and automatically pull their MOT history, mileage, and advisories directly into their digital garage.
