Important Notice: CarExplain is an educational and informational platform. It is not a garage, a mechanic, or a provider of professional automotive services. By using this platform, you acknowledge that all outputs are probabilistic, not definitive.

1. Nature of the Service

CarExplain utilises Large Language Models (LLMs) and deterministic rule-sets to assist in symptom analysis and garage quote interpretation. Because this technology is probabilistic and cannot physically interact with your vehicle, you must understand the following strict limitations:

  • Not a Physical Inspection: Artificial Intelligence cannot physically see, hear, smell, or test your vehicle. Our reports are generated entirely based on the text you provide. A physical mechanic uses thousands of sensory inputs (sound, vibration, smell, diagnostic codes) that an AI cannot access.
  • No Definitive Diagnosis: CarExplain can never provide a definitive mechanical diagnosis. Several faults can produce identical symptoms. The actual cause may require physical inspection, dismantling, or specialist diagnostic equipment.

2. Specific Disclaimers for Our Tools

2.1 AI Diagnostics (What's Wrong With My Car?)

Our diagnostic tool provides a structured analysis of potential causes. It does not confirm a fault. If our system escalates a symptom as "urgent" or "safety-critical," this is a conservative safety warning, not a verified mechanical finding. If you feel your vehicle is unsafe to drive, you must stop driving immediately, regardless of what the AI report states.

2.2 Quote Checker

Our Quote Checker provides an indicative breakdown of parts, labour, and VAT based on typical UK market rates. It is designed to help you ask informed questions, not to accuse a garage of misconduct. We are not liable for any financial decisions, negotiations, or disputes arising from the use of the Quote Checker. Prices vary significantly based on vehicle make, model, engine, region, parts quality (OEM vs. aftermarket), and garage type (independent vs. main dealer).

4. Your Statutory Rights (UK Consumer Law)

Nothing in this disclaimer affects your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. Under this Act, services must be performed with "reasonable care and skill"[reference:0]. However, CarExplain does not supply mechanical services; we supply digital information. Therefore, the "reasonable care and skill" standard applies to the provision of our software, not to the physical repair of your vehicle.

Furthermore, under the Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008, traders must include VAT in any indicated price and distinguish between an "estimate" and a "quotation"[reference:1][reference:2]. Our Quote Checker is designed to help you identify whether a garage has complied with these requirements.
