AI Disclaimer

Last Updated: October 2026

CarExplain utilises Large Language Models (LLMs) and deterministic rule-sets to assist in symptom analysis. Because this technology is probabilistic and not a physical mechanic, you must understand the following strict limitations.

1. Not a Physical Inspection

Artificial Intelligence cannot physically see, hear, smell, or test your vehicle. Our reports are generated entirely based on the text answers you provide. A physical mechanic uses thousands of sensory inputs (sound, vibration, smell, diagnostic codes) that an AI cannot access. Therefore, CarExplain can never provide a definitive mechanical diagnosis.

2. Hallucinations and Errors

AI models are capable of "hallucinating"—producing information that sounds convincing but is factually incorrect. While we use strict system prompts and structural guardrails to minimise this, we cannot guarantee that every possibility listed in your report is relevant or accurate for your specific make and model. Always verify with a professional.

3. Safety-Critical Escalation

We have programmed our system with a "Safety Gate." If your symptoms match severe, life-threatening faults (such as brake loss, steering failure, heavy smoke, or severe overheating), the system is designed to advise you to stop driving immediately. However, AI can miss context. If you feel unsafe driving your vehicle, stop driving, regardless of what the AI report says.

4. Data & Model Training

When we connect to third-party AI providers, we send your anonymised symptom data to generate the response. We explicitly configure our API integrations so that your data is not used to train public models. Your personal details, registration plate, and vehicle history remain private.

5. No Professional Advice

The outputs provided by CarExplain do not constitute professional automotive advice. The platform is an educational tool designed to empower you to have better conversations with garages. You are solely responsible for any decisions or financial commitments you make regarding your vehicle.
