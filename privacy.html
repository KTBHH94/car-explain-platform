Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

CarExplain ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your data when you use the CarExplain platform, in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who We Are

CarExplain is the data controller for the personal information processed through this platform. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, you can contact us at hello@carexplain.co.uk.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide you with a functional digital garage and diagnostic tool:

  • Vehicle Data: Make, model, year, registration, mileage, and fuel type.
  • Diagnostic Data: The symptoms you report, the answers you provide to our structured questions, and the resulting AI-generated reports.
  • Repair History: Records of parts replaced, costs, garage names, and outcomes you choose to save.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device type, and standard server logs (e.g., IP address) for security and performance monitoring.

3. How We Use Your Information

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases to process your data:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provide the diagnostic service and save your vehicle history to your account.
  • Legitimate Interest: To improve our platform, prevent fraud, and enhance the accuracy of our diagnostic tools.
  • Consent: Where you have explicitly opted in to receive marketing or product updates (we do not do this by default).

4. Data Storage & Security

In our current beta phase, the vehicle and diagnostic data you enter is stored locally within your browser (using LocalStorage). It is not transmitted to our servers. This means if you clear your browser data, your garage history will be deleted. When we transition to cloud accounts, your data will be securely encrypted and stored using enterprise-grade infrastructure providers (such as Supabase and Cloudflare).

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. When AI features are fully enabled, your anonymised symptom data (never your registration or personal details) may be sent to an AI provider (such as OpenAI or Anthropic) solely to generate your diagnostic report. We ensure all third-party processors comply with strict data protection standards.

6. Your Data Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure of your data (the "Right to be Forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Request the transfer of your data to another service (Data Portability).

You can exercise your right to deletion at any time by clicking "Remove Vehicle" in your garage. For other requests, please contact hello@carexplain.co.uk.

7. Cookies and Analytics

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep the website functioning. We may also use privacy-first analytics to understand aggregate user behaviour (e.g., which symptom categories are most common). We do not use invasive cross-site tracking cookies.
