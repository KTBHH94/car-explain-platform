Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Summary: We collect the minimum amount of data required to run your digital garage and AI diagnostics. We do not sell your data. We use enterprise-grade providers (Supabase, Cloudflare, Google) to secure it.

CarExplain ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your data when you use the CarExplain platform, in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data Controller Information

CarExplain is the "Data Controller" for the personal information processed through this platform. This means we are responsible for deciding how your data is used. If you have any questions about this policy, or wish to exercise your data rights, you can contact us at: hello@carexplain.co.uk.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide you with a functional digital garage, diagnostic tool, and community experience:

  • Account Data: Your email address and encrypted password (managed securely by Supabase Auth).
  • Vehicle Data: Make, model, year, registration, mileage, and fuel type.
  • Diagnostic & Quote Data: The symptoms you report, the answers you provide, and the resulting AI-generated reports or quote analyses.
  • Community Data: Any posts or replies you publish on the Community Forum.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device type, and IP address for security, rate-limiting, and performance monitoring.

3. How We Use Your Information (Legal Bases)

Under the UK GDPR, we must have a lawful basis to process your data. We rely on the following:

  • Contractual Necessity: To create your account, save your vehicle history, and generate your AI diagnostic reports.
  • Legitimate Interest: To prevent fraud, monitor API usage (to stop abuse of the AI), and improve the accuracy of our diagnostic tools.
  • Consent: Where you have explicitly opted in to receive marketing emails (we do not do this by default).

4. Data Storage, Security & Cloud Infrastructure

We take data security extremely seriously. Your data is not stored locally on your device. It is stored in an encrypted PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase.

All traffic between your browser and our database is routed through secure, encrypted Cloudflare Workers. We enforce strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies at the database level, ensuring that User A cannot access User B's private vehicle or repair records.

5. Third-Party Data Processors

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers. However, to run the platform, we share specific data with trusted "Data Processors":

  • Supabase: Database hosting and authentication.
  • Cloudflare: Website hosting and secure backend API routing.
  • Google (Gemini AI): When you use the AI Diagnostic or Quote Checker, your symptom text and quote text are sent to Google's API to generate the response. We explicitly configure our API integration so that your data is NOT used to train public AI models. We do not send your email address or registration plate to the AI.
  • Resend: To deliver transactional emails (like password resets).

6. International Data Transfers

Our primary infrastructure is located within the UK and the European Economic Area (EEA). Where data is transferred outside the UK (for example, to US-based AI servers or cloud providers), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Your Data Rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have several rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can correct inaccurate data via your account settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can delete your vehicles, diagnostic reports, or your entire account at any time. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently wiped from our active databases.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can ask us to pause the processing of your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Complain: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use strictly necessary storage (browser LocalStorage) to keep you logged in and secure your session. We do not use invasive cross-site tracking cookies, third-party advertising pixels, or sell data to ad networks.

9. Children's Privacy

CarExplain is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by placing a prominent notice on our website or sending you an email. Your continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of England and Wales. If you reside in Scotland or Northern Ireland, you may also benefit from mandatory provisions of the local law in your country of residence.
