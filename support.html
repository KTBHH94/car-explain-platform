Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Your data is securely stored in our encrypted cloud database (hosted by Supabase). Because your account is tied to your email address, you can log in from any device (phone, tablet, or laptop) and your digital garage will be exactly as you left it.

How do I delete my account or data?

Under UK GDPR, you have the "Right to be Forgotten". You can delete individual vehicles and diagnostic reports directly within your Garage. If you wish to delete your entire account permanently, please email us at hello@carexplain.co.uk from the email address associated with your account.

Is CarExplain free to use?

Yes, the core diagnostic tool, digital garage, and community are free to use. Free accounts receive up to 3 AI diagnostic reports per month. If you require unlimited reports and advanced features, you can upgrade to CarExplain Premium.

Can CarExplain fix my car?

No. CarExplain is an informational and educational tool designed to help you understand symptoms and ask better questions before visiting a garage. We do not perform physical repairs. You must always use a qualified mechanic for physical work.

Are the repair prices in the Quote Checker guaranteed?

No. The Quote Checker provides an indicative range based on average UK labour rates and parts costs. Prices vary significantly based on your location, the specific vehicle, and whether the garage uses OEM or aftermarket parts. Always obtain a written, itemized quote from your garage.

Report a Technical Bug

If you encounter a technical issue on the platform, please email us with the following details so our developers can fix it quickly:

  • The device and browser you are using (e.g., iPhone 14 / Safari, or Windows 11 / Chrome).
  • A detailed description of what you were doing when the error occurred.
  • A screenshot of the error message (if possible).
