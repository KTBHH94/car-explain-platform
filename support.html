Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
In our current beta phase, all vehicle and diagnostic data is stored securely in your own browser (using LocalStorage). We cannot see it, and it is not on our servers. This means if you clear your browser cache or switch to a different device, your garage will appear empty.

Is CarExplain free to use?
The diagnostic tool and digital garage are completely free during our public beta phase. We may introduce premium features (like automated MOT lookups or advanced quote analysis) in the future, but the core diagnostic tool will remain accessible.

Can CarExplain fix my car?
No. CarExplain is an informational tool designed to help you understand symptoms and ask better questions. You must always use a qualified mechanic for physical repairs.

Report a Bug

If you encounter a technical issue, please email us with the following details so we can fix it quickly:

  • The device and browser you are using (e.g., iPhone 14 / Safari, or Windows 11 / Chrome).
  • A description of what you were doing when the error occurred.
  • A screenshot of the error message (if possible).
